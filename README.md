# ✈️ White Cloud Airlines — Performance Dashboard

> **Turning airline data into simple, decision-ready insights.**

The **White Cloud Airlines Performance Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to give a quick, clear view of airline revenue, profitability, passenger activity, and flight delays.

It is built for **business users, managers, and decision-makers** who want to understand airline performance without needing to work directly with raw data.

## 🔎 What Can You Learn From This Dashboard?

The dashboard is designed to answer practical questions such as:

- Which routes generate the most revenue?
- Which route is the most profitable?
- How does revenue change from day to day?
- How is profit divided between domestic and international operations?
- Which region experiences higher average delays?
- How many passengers are typically carried on each flight?
- Does performance change when looking at a specific route or region?

In short, it helps turn **airline operational data into a story that is easy to understand.**

The **Daily Revenue Trend** shows how revenue changes over time.

This makes it easier to spot:

- 📈 High-revenue days
- 📉 Low-revenue days
- 🔄 Changes and fluctuations in performance

This view is useful for understanding whether revenue is relatively stable or driven by occasional spikes.

## 💡 Why This Dashboard Matters

Airline performance involves many moving parts:

**Revenue → Routes → Passengers → Profit → Delays**

Looking at these areas together provides a better understanding of overall performance.

For example:

> A route may generate strong revenue, but frequent delays could still indicate an operational challenge.

This dashboard helps users look beyond a single number and understand the bigger picture.

## 🚀 How to Use the Dashboard

### For a non-technical user

1. Open the Power BI dashboard.
2. Start with the four KPI cards at the top.
3. Look at the **Daily Revenue Trend** to understand revenue movement.
4. Review **Revenue by Route** to identify high-performing routes.
5. Check **Profit Share by Region** to compare domestic and international performance.
6. Review **Average Delay by Region** to understand operational delays.
7. Use the **Route** and **Region** filters to explore specific areas.

That's it — no coding is required.
